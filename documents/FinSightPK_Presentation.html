FinSight PK

Regime-Aware, Sentiment-Augmented KSE-100 Forecasting with Explainability & Web Deployment

Financial AI NLP Time-Series Forecasting Explainable AI KSE-100
Problem Statement
Pakistani stock market mein koi AI-assisted forecasting tool kyun nahi hai?
📉

Koi AI Tooling Nahi

Pakistani retail investors, portfolio managers, aur fintech firms KSE-100 decisions karte hain bina kisi AI-based forecasting system ke. Global level par aise systems exist karte hain — Pakistan ke liye nahi.

🗞️

Pakistani Financial News Dataset Nahi

Koi labeled Pakistani financial news sentiment dataset nahi hai. Existing models English ya Indian data use karte hain — jo Pakistan ke market context ko accurately reflect nahi karta.

🐂🐻

Regime Context Ignore Hota Hai

Existing KSE-100 models Bull aur Bear market mein same features aur same model use karte hain — yeh fundamentally galat hai. Market ka behavior dono conditions mein alag hota hai.

🔲

Black Box Predictions

Jo models bhi exist karte hain, woh prediction dete hain lekin explain nahi karte ke kyun. Financial decisions ke liye explanation zaruri hai — isliye Explainable AI (XAI) crucial hai.

💡 FinSight PK Solution: Ek end-to-end AI pipeline jo NLP (news sentiment), regime detection, hybrid deep learning, aur explainability ko combine karta hai — aur ek publicly accessible web application ke through deliver karta hai.
Overview
Step 1 — Pakistani Financial Sentiment Dataset
Duniya ka pehla labeled Pakistani financial news sentiment corpus banana
A
News Headlines Scrape Karna Web Scraping

Kya karte hain: Dawn Business (dawn.com/business) aur Profit.pk — Pakistan ke top 2 financial news sources — se headlines scrape karte hain. Python mein BeautifulSoup ya Scrapy use karte hain.

Kitni headlines: Approx 1,500 headlines — yeh amount NLP research ke liye standard hai initial fine-tuning ke liye.

🛠️ Tool: Python + BeautifulSoup / Scrapy library. Har headline ke saath date bhi store karte hain taake baad mein daily score bana sakein.
B
Manual Annotation — Label Studio Annotation

Kya karte hain: Label Studio (free tool) mein har headline ko 3 categories mein label karte hain:

Positive ✓"KSE-100 surges 800 points on foreign inflows"
Negative ✗"State Bank raises interest rates to 22%"
Negative ✗"IMF deal delayed amid political uncertainty"
Neutral —"PSX trading volume remains flat today"
Positive ✓"Pakistan receives $3B in foreign reserves boost"
🤝 Inter-Annotator Agreement: Teeno team members alag alag annotate karte hain, phir Cohen's Kappa Score se check karte hain ke agreement kitna consistent hai. Kappa > 0.8 = excellent. Jahan disagreement ho — discuss karke decide karte hain.

Pehle ek Annotation Guidebook banate hain jisme rules clearly defined hain — jaise "interest rates barhna = Negative", "IMF deal = Negative", "foreign investment = Positive".

C
DistilBERT Fine-Tuning Deep Learning

DistilBERT kya hai: Yeh ek transformer-based language model hai — ChatGPT jaisi technology ka lightweight version. HuggingFace se available hai, free mein.

Fine-tuning kya hai: Hum is pre-trained model ko apne Pakistani financial news data par train karte hain taake yeh Pakistan ke specific financial language aur context samjhe.

Data split: 80% training, 10% validation, 10% testing — standard ML practice.

📊 Output: Har trading day ke liye ek Daily Sentiment Score:

Sentiment Score = Positive Ratio − Negative Ratio

Example: Agar 10 headlines mein 6 positive, 3 negative → Score = 0.6 − 0.3 = +0.3

Step 2 — Market Regime Detection
K-Means Clustering se har trading day ko Bull / Bear / Sideways label karna
A
KSE-100 Price Data Download Data Collection

Source: Yahoo Finance ya PSX (Pakistan Stock Exchange) se KSE-100 ka daily OHLCV data download karte hain — 2015 se aaj tak.

OHLCV matlab: Open, High, Low, Close, Volume — yeh standard price data format hai.

🔧 Tool: Python mein yfinance library — ek line mein sara data download ho jata hai: yf.download("^KSE100", start="2015-01-01")
B
Rolling Statistics Calculate Karna Feature Engineering

Har trading day ke liye 2 values calculate karte hain (20-day window use karke):

1. Rolling Average Return — pichle 20 dinon ka average return. Positive = market upar ja rahi thi, negative = neeche.

2. Rolling Volatility — pichle 20 dinon mein returns kitne vary hue. High = zyada uncertain market.

📈 In 2 numbers (return, volatility) se hum har day ko ek point bana lete hain 2D space mein. Yeh points cluster karne ke liye tayar hain.
C
K-Means Clustering (k=3) Unsupervised ML

K-Means kya karta hai: Yeh automatically data points ko groups mein divide karta hai — bina kisi pre-labeling ke. Hum k=3 set karte hain yaani 3 groups.

Clusters automatically ban jaate hain:

🟢
Bull
High return + Low volatility. Market consistently upar ja rahi hai.
🔴
Bear
Negative return + High volatility. Market neeche aur uncertain hai.
🟡
Sideways
Near-zero return + Moderate volatility. Market flat hai.
✅ Validation: Cluster quality check karte hain Elbow Method aur Silhouette Score se. Phir yeh regime labels one-hot encode karke Step 3 ke LSTM mein feature ke tor par dete hain.

Yeh FinSight PK ka sabse novel component hai — kisi bhi prior KSE-100 paper ne regime labels directly forecasting model mein nahi daale.

📊 EPU (Economic Policy Uncertainty) Index kya hai: Yeh measure karta hai ke Pakistan mein economic policy kitni uncertain hai. Example: IMF negotiations ke waqt EPU high hota hai. Yeh monthly data hota hai jise daily forward-fill karte hain.
B
LSTM Model — Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? Deep Learning

LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory) kya hai: Yeh ek special neural network hai jo time-series data ke liye design kiya gaya hai. Yeh past patterns "yaad" rakh sakta hai — isliye stock market jaise sequential data ke liye best hai.

30-day sliding window: Model pichle 30 dinon ka sara data dekhta hai aur phir next price predict karta hai. Jaise agar aaj 17 June hai toh model 18 May se 16 June ka data dekhega.

⚙️ Training Details:
— Loss Function: MSE (Mean Squared Error) + MAE
— Dropout Regularization: Overfitting rokne ke liye
— Early Stopping: Training automatically rok deta hai jab improvement ruk jaye
— Monte Carlo Dropout: Prediction uncertainty estimate karta hai (confidence interval)

Step 4 — SHAP Explainability (XAI)
"Model ne yeh predict kyun kiya?" — har regime ke liye alag alag explain karna
A
Problem — Black Box Models Problem

Deep learning models black box hote hain: Model prediction deta hai lekin explain nahi karta ke kyun. Financial decisions mein yeh unacceptable hai — investor ko pata hona chahiye ke yeh prediction kis cheez ki wajah se aayi.

❓ Example: Model keh raha hai "KSE-100 agle 7 din mein 2% giregi" — lekin kyun? Kya yeh news sentiment ki wajah se hai? Ya EPU index badh gaya? Ya RSI overbought zone mein hai?
B
SHAP kya hai? Solution

SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations): Yeh game theory se inspired ek technique hai jo har input feature ko ek contribution score assign karta hai — har individual prediction ke liye.

Simple matlab: SHAP bata deta hai ke "is specific prediction mein, sentiment ne +15% contribute kiya, EPU ne +40% contribute kiya, RSI ne −10% contribute kiya."

🔬 Technical Implementation: shap.DeepExplainer use karte hain trained LSTM model par. Yeh specifically deep learning models ke liye design kiya gaya hai.
